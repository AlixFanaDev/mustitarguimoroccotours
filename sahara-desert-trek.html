Merzouga · Erg Chebbi

Most travellers remember the desert long after they leave Morocco. It is the sound of a camel stepping through soft sand at golden hour, the music around a fire, and a night sky that feels close enough to reach for.

We run this experience privately from our home base in Merzouga — at the very edge of Erg Chebbi. There is no fixed group, no rush: the days below are the shape we travel most, and we adapt the pacing to your group.

Itinerary outline

Day 1

Merzouga — camel trek into Erg Chebbi

Meet in Merzouga in the late afternoon. Ride camels up over the dunes as the light softens, reach the camp, and settle in for a tagine dinner and Berber music around the fire.

Day 2

Dune sunrise, Khamlia & the desert edge

Up before dawn to watch the sun flood the dunes. After breakfast and sandboarding, loop the desert edge: Khamlia for Gnawa music, a visit with a nomad family, the seasonal lake and the palmgroves of Rissani. Second night back at camp or in a Merzouga hotel — your call.

Day 3

Rissani souk & the road home

Morning trek out of the dunes, then the old souk of Rissani and the Mausoleum of Moulay Ali Cherif before we transfer you onward — to Fes, Marrakech, or wherever your route continues.

Day 4 optional

Extend toward the Dades Valley

Add a fourth day to thread onto the Dades–Todra scenery, or simply take a slower desert morning. This version pairs naturally with a road trip from either Fes or Marrakech.

Accommodation

Standard nights are in a private tented camp: proper mattresses, blankets and a shared bathroom block. If you’d rather not give up comfort, our VIP camp adds private ensuite tents, hot showers and a higher standard of dinner.

Price guidance

From 950 MAD per person for 2 days / 1 night (share basis, two guests). The per-person price comes down as your group grows and moves up slightly in peak season. Tell us your dates and group size and we’ll send an exact quote — usually within two hours.

Good to know

Is the trek suitable for families and older travellers?

Yes. The ride is gentle and we adjust the pace and distance to your group. Camels are led, never rushed, and you can rest at the camp whenever you like.

What happens in extreme weather?

Safety comes first. In rain or a heavy sandstorm we swap the sand section for an adapted route or a desert 4x4, and always keep the camp experience intact.

Can we sleep in a more comfortable camp?

Yes. Standard tents include mattresses and blankets; the upgrade to a VIP camp adds private ensuite tents, hot showers and a higher standard of dinner.

Can the trip be longer or shorter than three days?

Yes — we run 2, 3 and 4 day versions of this route, and the day-by-day plan adapts to your arrival and departure times.
