Atlas · Sahara · Cities · Coast

A private road trip is the most flexible way to see Morocco. You decide where to linger and where to move on; a driver-guide handles the roads, the rooms and the daily logistics, and the small detours — the bread oven, the cricket song, the mint tea invitation — happen because the person driving is from here.

The routes below are starting shapes, not fixed packages. Tell us how long you have, where you land and what you love, and we’ll draft an itinerary you can reshape freely.

Popular route shapes

6–7 days

South loop: Marrakech → Merzouga → Fes

Aït Benhaddou and the High Atlas, the Dades Valley, two nights in the Merzouga desert, then the Ziz valley road north to Fes.

Classic: Fes → Merzouga → Marrakech

The reverse classic — out of Fes over the Middle Atlas to the dunes, then back via Ouarzazate and the Atlas passes.

10–12 days

Grand tour: coast to desert to coast

Casablanca – Rabat – Fes – Merzouga – Dades – Marrakech – Essaouira: cities, desert and a breather on the Atlantic before heading home.

Accommodation

We match rooms to the budget tier you choose: comfortable guesthouses and riads, higher-end boutique riads, or premium hotels and luxury desert camps. You set the level; we do the legwork.

Price guidance

From 1,150 MAD per day per person (share basis, two guests, standard accommodation). Every element — nights, miles, meals included — is itemised on your quote so you can see exactly what you’re paying for.

Good to know

Which city can we start from?

Anywhere you land — Marrakech, Fes, Casablanca and Tangier are the most common, and we adjust the loop to fit.

Can we travel with children?

Yes, families are very welcome. We fit car seats, pace drives generously and choose camps and riads that are child-friendly.

Can we choose our own budget level?

That is the point of a private trip. Pick a standard, comfortable, or premium tier and we build the route around it.

What if we want to change the plan mid-trip?

Your guide is flexible by design. Most changes — an extra stop, a later morning — are handled simply by telling us the night before.
