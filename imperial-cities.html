Fes · Meknès · Rabat · Marrakech

Each of Morocco’s four imperial cities keeps a different rhythm. Fes is the oldest — a living medina of workshops and hidden squares. Meknès is grand and relaxed, Rabat is elegant and by the sea, and Marrakech closes the loop with its famous dusk energy.

We connect them in a private, unhurried loop with a driver-guide who stays with you, so the distance between the cities — stops, lunches, and local context included — is part of the tour too.

Itinerary outline

Day 1

Fes — medina, tanneries & artisans

Arrive and begin with a walking route through the old medina: the tanneries, the Bou Inania madrasa, and the copper and wood workshops. Dinner in a local riad.

Day 2

Meknès & Volubilis

Moulay Ismail’s granaries and soaring gate, then the Roman ruins of Volubilis before pushing on to Rabat for the night.

Day 3

Rabat — kasbah & tower

Morning along the Kasbah of the Udayas and the Hassan Tower, then a relaxed afternoon by the river before heading south.

Day 4 optional

Marrakech, via Casablanca

If you want it, a loop through Casablanca’s Hassan II mosque; otherwise drive straight to Marrakech and finish with Djemaa el-Fna at dusk.

Add-ons

Chefchaouen or Essaouira

Stretch the loop to 6 days with the blue city of Chefchaouen after Fes, or to 8 with a coastal stop at Essaouira and a slower pace throughout.

Good to know

Which city does the tour start in?

The standard itinerary starts in Fes and ends in Marrakech, but the direction and the start city are easy to reverse.

Can we add Chefchaouen or Essaouira?

Yes. The blue city of Chefchaouen fits naturally after Fes, and Essaouira can be added at the Marrakech end. Both add a day or two.

What level of accommodation is included?

Our standard level uses well-located riads and 4-star hotels with breakfast. Upgrades to 5-star are available on request.

Are entrance fees included?

Not in the base price. Your guide will handle tickets, and you only pay for what you choose to enter.

Is the tour private?

It is private to your group — you share the vehicle and guide only with the people you travel with.
